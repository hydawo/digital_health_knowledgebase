## 1. Zawada 2025, INCLUDE

- File: Europe PMC JATS XML, PMC12381640, plus mmc1.docx and the CONSORT figure image
- First author (JATS byline): Stephanie Zawada, Mayo Clinic College of Medicine and Science. Senior author Bart Demaerschalk.
- Year / journal: 2025, *Mayo Clinic Proceedings: Digital Health* 3(3):100240
- DOI: 10.1016/j.mcpdig.2025.100240. PMCID: PMC12381640 (PMID 40881107)
- Platform per Methods (quote): "Participants downloaded the HIPAA-compliant Beiwe application at baseline." Matches the queue and Q124. Forest (Oak, Jasmine) for processing. No wearable.
- Verdict: INCLUDE. Real cohort with an itemised CONSORT funnel, a lost-to-follow-up definition, survey adherence, per-participant passive completeness and named failure modes.
- Existing profile on same cohort: none. The authors' earlier *Sensors* 2024 paper (DOI 10.3390/s24113595, cited as ref 17) is a design-considerations paper and was not screened here.
- Profile: `profiles/beiwe-stroke-tia-mood-2024-window.md`

Extraction (section references are to the published article headings and supplement labels).

- Design. Prospective cohort, proof of concept, single centre (Patients and Methods, Study Design and Population). IRB 22-009345.
- Funding / COI. Mayo Clinic, P.E.O. Scholar Award, Kosciuszko Foundation award, PhRMA Foundation fellowship, NCATS TL1 5TL1TR002380-05 (Footnotes). Only COI statement concerns two editorial board members (Potential Competing Interests). Compensation not reported.
- Population. Mayo Clinic Hospital, Arizona. IS, TIA or other transient stroke-like symptoms at discharge, plus prior IS or TIA in sequelae monitoring. Prior dementia excluded. Of 44 downloaders, 59.1% male, mean age 57, 88.6% White, iOS 34 (77.3%), Android 10 (22.7%) (Table 2).
- Funnel (Figure). 67 recruited, 54 enrolled, 44 downloaded, 38 completed first MADRS, 35 completed. Reasons per step in the profile. 35/54 = 64.8% (Abstract, Results).
- Analytic exclusions. 1 for Forest quality threshold (Results, Feasibility). 15 for missing measures, "primarily due to GPS measures" (Results, Prediction Models).
- Duration. 8 weeks, up to 90 days captured (Data Collection). Recruitment 1 March to 31 August 2024. Monitoring 1 March to 15 November 2024 (Abstract).
- Deployment model. BYOD, remote consent and download, recruited by email after discharge. Accelerometer 10 s on, 10 s off. GPS 1 min on, 10 min off (Data Collection).
- Retention definition. Lost to follow-up if the phone "stopped streaming data" or the participant was "no longer responsive" (Data Collection).
- Survey adherence. "73% of participants completing 8 or more weeks of PHQ-8 surveys" (Results). Denominator not stated. Supplemental Figure 3 histogram.
- Passive completeness. Supplemental Table 2, per participant, 43 rows. GPS 34 numeric, 9 missing, range 2.9 to 42.4. Accelerometer 41 numeric, 2 missing, range 13.3% to 94.3%. No summary statistic printed. Column headers say "completeness" while the text describes minutes off or missing per day. Denominator not stated. 4 GPS and 14 accelerometer values exceed the duty-cycle ceiling implied by the stated configuration.
- OS breakdown of completeness. Not reported.
- Heartbeat. Not stated. No mention of heartbeat, keepalive, server version or hosting. 171 of the 260 calendar days of the monitoring window fall on or after 2024-05-29. Per-participant enrolment dates not reported.
- Technical failure modes. Download issue (3), smartphone issue (1 dropout), GPS loss with causes listed but unquantified (Supplemental Table 2 text), rural connectivity and travel (Discussion), older phones and unreliable cellular networks (Discussion).
- Internal inconsistency. Supplemental Table 3 totals 41 (3 at 30 days, 38 at 60 and 90 days) against 35 completers in the CONSORT figure.
- Authors' conclusions. Smartphone sensors captured behaviour measures for at least 8 weeks. Low data proportions may bias averages. Future protocols should reduce missingness, possibly testing Apple Watch GPS (Discussion).
- Not reported. Compensation, reminder or support contacts, Beiwe hosting and version, heartbeat status, completeness denominator, OS-split completeness, per-participant enrolment dates, PHQ-8 completion denominator.
