## 2. Pinto 2026 (webMFT), REJECT (no-cohort)

- File: Europe PMC JATS XML, PMC13613989
- First author (byline): Bernardine M. Pinto. Co-authors Kindred, DeScenza, Dunsiger.
- Year / journal: 2026, *Psycho-Oncology* 35(9):e70616
- DOI: 10.1002/pon.70616. PMCID: PMC13613989
- Platform per Methods (quote): "Upon completion of baseline assessments, participants in both groups received a Fitbit tracker to monitor their activity." Fitbit matches the queue. An ActiGraph was used only at baseline and post-intervention for screening and the primary outcome.
- Verdict: REJECT, no-cohort. Settles the queue conflict in favour of the 2026-10-05 rejection. The cohort is real (61 randomised, 30 analysed in the webMFT arm), but the paper is a secondary analysis of psychosocial constructs. It reports no Fitbit wear time, no sync or data completeness, no attrition and no technical failures. Its only operational figure, "on average, 90% of calls were completed," is cross-cited from the parent trial. Weekly text reminders to sync are described but not quantified.
- One deployment-relevant observation, not enough to include. Fitbit active minutes in week 1 averaged 150.83 min/week although ActiGraph had screened participants as sedentary. The authors flag a possible ceiling effect.
- Lead. The parent RCT, Pinto et al., *J Cancer Surviv* 2025, DOI 10.1007/s11764-025-01811-7, may carry the deployment figures. Not screened here.
- Existing profile on same cohort: none.

## 3. Culhane 2026, INCLUDE

- File: Europe PMC JATS XML, PMC13592238, plus Multimedia Appendix 1 (formative-v10-e88466-s001.docx) from the Europe PMC supplementary files. PDF not retrievable. PMC and Europe PMC PDF endpoints both served a browser check.
- First author (JATS author list, checked against the Authors' Contributions statement): Brien William Culhane. Senior author Justin Taylor Baker. Ivan Steenstra appears in the JATS contributor block but is not in the contributions statement. Treated as the handling editor, per the known JMIR byline hazard.
- Year / journal: 2026, *JMIR Formative Research* 10:e88466, published 2026-09-21
- DOI: 10.2196/88466. PMCID: PMC13592238
- Platform per Methods (quote): "Sleep and activity were monitored using waterproof, wrist-worn GENEActiv devices (Activinsights Ltd)... Devices sampled at 20 Hz for up to 30 days." Matches the queue. No phenotyping platform.
- Verdict: INCLUDE. Real deployment on a 21-bed inpatient unit with an admission-to-offer-to-acceptance funnel, recorded refusal reasons, device-loss and latency failure modes and the authors' design recommendations.
- Existing profile on same cohort: none. Same lab and pipeline (DPSleep, Rahimi-Eichi, Baker) as `geneactiv-beiwe-college-year-deep-phenotyping.md`, but a different population.

Extraction (section references are to the published article headings).

- Design. Two-phase implementation, iCHECK-DH reporting. IRB classed it as not human subject research. Verbal consent in routine workflow (Ethical Considerations).
- Funding / COI. Discretionary McLean ITP funds, no external grant (Budget Planning; Footnotes). Baker reports consulting fees and equity from Tetricus Labs (Conflicts of Interest). Medical director Yip, the main user, is an author. Patient compensation not reported.
- Setting. McLean Hospital, Massachusetts. 21-bed locked unit in a 238-bed hospital. Ages 18 to 65. Mean stay 17 d (SD 23). n=181 unit patients 36.5% female, 58.6% male, 5.0% unreported. Race White 145 (80) (Table 1).
- Funnel, phase 1, Aug to Dec 2023 (Coverage). 155 admitted, 88 (56.8%) offered, 68 (77.3%) accepted, 42/68 (61.8%) had at least one report delivered.
- Refusals (Patient and Staff Experience). 20 recorded reasons. 5 (25%) privacy or surveillance. The rest "general unwillingness to participate without particular reason."
- Phase 2, Jan to Jun 2024 (Coverage). Uptake not tracked. 34 reports, 32 (94.1%) requested by the medical director, 2 by rotating clinicians, 0 by the third psychiatrist.
- First request model (Appendix 1). "only six report requests were completed."
- Turnaround (Coverage). About 5 days to under 24 hours after full automation. Some same-day delivery.
- Deployment model. Provisioned. 30 GENEActiv Original devices from lab inventory at about US $275 each (Budget Planning). Offline. RA collects the device before data exist (Table 2).
- Technical failure modes. Latency from nonnetworked collection and in-development software (Discussion). One device taken home and mailed back, none destroyed (Sustainability). Interpretability mismatch between percentiles and nursing language (Barriers and Facilitators). Psychiatrist turnover before phase 2 (Coverage).
- Not reported. Wear time, valid nights, sleep-detection failure, early device removal, per-patient days worn, any data-quality exclusion, reasons the 67 non-offered admissions were not offered, patient compensation.
- Authors' conclusions. Technically feasible. Utility concentrated in one early adopter. Needs EMR integration, "last-night" metrics before rounds, wireless near-real-time devices and champions at every staff level (Conclusions; Table 3).
