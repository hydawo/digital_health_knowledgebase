## 1. Ghazi et al. 2026, BMJ Open

- Source read: Europe PMC full-text XML (PMC13691778), CC BY-NC 4.0. XML only.
- First author: Lama Ghazi (first `contrib-type="author"` record; all 9 contributor records are typed author). Not checked against a PDF byline. Year 2026. BMJ Open 2026;16(10):e121681. Epub 2026-10-22. DOI 10.1136/bmjopen-2026-121681. PMID 42914550. PMCID PMC13691778 (from the XML).
- Deployed (Methods, Remote BP monitoring): "Participants randomised to the RBPM arm were provided with a validated, Food and Drug Administration (FDA)-approved upper-arm home BP device (Withings BPM Connect Pro), which automatically transmits readings via Wi-Fi/Bluetooth to the EHR and was programmed to transmit data directly to the study REDCap database." Matches the queue. Withings is profiled in Module 1 (`module-01-wearables/profiles/withings.md`, which lists BPM Connect Pro under the Transtek 510(k) K223374).

### Extraction

- Design: two-arm parallel-group pilot RCT, 1:1, permuted blocks of 2 and 4 in REDCap. Open-label, no blinding (Methods, Study design; Randomisation and allocation). ClinicalTrials.gov NCT06247111. UAB IRB-300012428 (Ethics approval).
- Funding/COI: NIH Forge AHEAD P50MD017338 and BIGDATA MHI Core P30AR072583 (funding metadata). Competing interests "None declared." No Withings funding or Withings-affiliated author named. Who paid for the devices is not reported.
- Compensation (Ethics approval): US$25 at consent and US$50 at the 12-week visit (US$75 usual care). RBPM arm received US$30 per pharmacist visit attended (US$435 total).
- Population/setting: UAB Hospital Kirklin Clinic ED postdischarge clinic, Birmingham, Alabama, USA (Setting). Mean age 48.4 (SD 12.8), 38% female, 71% Black (Results, Baseline; Table 1). 79% (19/24) insured. 38% (9/24) reported difficulty paying for medical care. None had a PCP.
- Eligibility (Methods): age 18 or over, SBP 130 or more or DBP 80 or more and not above 160/100, English-speaking, smartphone with video capability, no PCP. Excluded pregnancy, breastfeeding, second or overnight shift work, prior research opt-out. The abstract states SBP 130 to 160.
- Enrolled to analysed (Results, Enrollment; Figure 2 caption; 12-week retention): 27 assessed, 24 met inclusion and were randomised (12 per arm), 24 completed baseline, 16 completed the 12-week visit (8 per arm) and were analysed for BP.
- Attrition reasons (12-week retention): RBPM arm, 1 safety withdrawal at week 3 (BP uncontrolled), 3 completed most weekly visits but did not attend or give BP at the 12-week visit. Usual care, 4 did not attend; reasons "not systematically captured".
- Duration: 12 weeks per participant. Enrolment window given as July 2024 to January 2025 (Abstract; Methods, Recruitment) and as July 2024 to March 2025 (Results, Enrollment).
- Deployment model: provisioned cuff, intervention arm only. Own smartphone for Doximity video visits. OS not reported.
- Data path: Withings to EHR and then to REDCap (Data collection and management). Methods also says the device "was programmed to transmit data directly to the study REDCap database". Connectivity is "Wi-Fi/Bluetooth" in Methods and "cellular-enabled ... requiring no Wi-Fi" in Discussion.
- Measurement protocol (Methods): twice in the morning and twice in the evening, "a total of four readings per day over 12 weeks." Daily automated text reminders. Study team contact after 3 consecutive missed days.
- Recruitment: research coordinator screened the EHR on prespecified clinic days only (Recruitment and screening). Refusals logged but the count is not given in the text.
- Recruitment rate: 24/27, 88.9% (Primary outcomes, Recruitment rate). Methods define it over eligible patients. Results compute it over those assessed.
- Retention: defined a priori as 12-week visit with clinic BP. 67% (16/24), 8/12 in each arm.
- Fidelity: 127 pharmacist visits across 12 RBPM participants. Mean 10.6 (SD 3.3) of 12, range 3 to 12. BP reviewed at 96.9% (123/127). Medications discussed at 89.8% (114/127). 30 medication management interventions, 23 of them dose escalations (Discussion).
- Device adherence (Home BP reading adherence): 11 of 12 (92%) transmitted at least one reading. 1 transmitted none "despite receiving the device." 2029 readings among the 11. Mean 169 (SD 115) per participant. "Expected maximum: 168 readings with twice-daily readings over 84 days." Mean 15 (SD 9) readings per week. Weekly n ranged 9 to 11 (Figure 3 caption).
- Acceptability: 8 of 12 RBPM participants completed AIM, IAM, FIM. AIM 4.69 (SD 0.88), IAM 4.75 (SD 0.71), FIM 4.78 (SD 0.41).
- Safety (Safety monitoring; 12-week retention): nursing staff reviewed readings Monday to Friday under the UAB Medicine RBPM programme. No red-alert readings requiring escalation beyond the one withdrawal.
- Technical failure modes: none reported. No sync, connectivity, cuff or app failures are named. The reason for the participant with no readings is not given.
- Authors' conclusions: "feasible, highly acceptable"; proceed to a multisite RCT. Recommendations: remote outcome ascertainment to cut attrition tied to the in-person visit; give the usual care arm a home cuff; tie compensation to shared milestones equally across arms.
- Not reported: refusal count; device cost and supplier; OS or phone type; whether the Withings app or a Withings account was needed; number of 3-day missed-reading contacts; per-week adherence against the stated four-a-day protocol; any data loss between Withings, the EHR and REDCap; supplementary table 1 content (not retrieved).
- Inconsistencies in the paper: enrolment end month (January vs March 2025); connectivity (Wi-Fi/Bluetooth vs cellular); readings per day (four vs twice-daily maximum of 168, with a mean of 169 above it); abstract baseline SBP for the RBPM arm (153.5, all 12) vs completer baseline (151.3); "FDA-approved" vs "FDA-cleared"; week-12 SBP printed as "141.7±2 70.4".
