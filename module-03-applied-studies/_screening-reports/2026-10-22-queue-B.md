## 1. Stahl et al. 2026

- File: Europe PMC full-text XML, PMC13626640 (no PDF stored, see below)
- First author: Sarah T Stahl, from the JATS author group. The publisher PDF could not be retrieved, so the PDF byline was not seen. The JATS lists the handling editor (MacNeill) after the four authors.
- Year 2026. *JMIR Formative Research* 10:e88773. DOI 10.2196/88773. PMCID PMC13626640.
- Device actually deployed (Procedures). "Participants were provided an Apple Watch Series 8 via the United States Postal Service and asked to wear it over the 6-week trial, except when they were bathing or showering, when we asked them to charge the watch." Matches the queue's Apple Watch guess.
- Verdict: INCLUDE. Real 21-person deployment with a recruitment funnel, a valid-day definition, valid-day counts by group, a mid-study protocol change caused by data-collection problems and an attendance count.
- Existing Module 3 profile covering this cohort: none.

Extraction
- Design. Single-arm pilot, NCT05309577, IRB STUDY21060122. Feasibility and acceptability primary.
- Funding/COI. NIH R41AG069596, P30AG024978. SFS owns and is CEO of Activity Rhythm Solutions, which developed myRhythmWatch. Compensation US $125.
- Population. Pittsburgh. 15 caregivers (mean age 61.78), 6 people with dementia (mean 81.45).
- Funnel (Methods, Participants). 25 caregivers screened, 3 ineligible, 7 not interested, 15 enrolled. 7 of 15 declined to involve the care recipient and 2 recipients were ineligible, giving 6. All 21 completed. 11 of 15 caregivers had pre and post measures.
- Duration. 6 weeks. Enrolment March 2023 to April 2024. People with dementia later limited to 1 month.
- Deployment. Provisioned watch by post. 18 of 21 virtual orientation, 3 in person. Troubleshooting call at 2 days as needed.
- Completeness (Results). Valid day is at least 20 hours. 21 of 21 met 3 consecutive valid days. Caregivers 35.5 valid days (SD 2.7). People with dementia 30.0 (SD 3.2).
- Technical failure modes. "Increasing data collection issues among people with dementia", not itemised. No device, sync or battery failures reported. No adverse events.
- Not reported. Phone ownership, iOS version, cause of the data problems, per-week completeness in text.
- Internal inconsistencies. Abstract says "only 6 were active users" of 6 people with dementia. Sex 80% vs 87%. Black 24% vs 26.7%. t 2.417 vs 2.477. 9 of 15 "not appropriate" in Discussion vs 7 plus 2 in Methods.
- PDF. Not stored. `formative.jmir.org/.../PDF` returned HTTP 202 with an empty body. `pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/.../pdf/` returned an HTML interstitial. Neither was pursued further.

## 2. Lu et al. 2026

- File: `literature/2026-lu-plosdigithealth-accelerometer-implementation-gerotherapeutic-trial-able.pdf` (magic bytes %PDF-, text extraction checked) plus Europe PMC XML.
- First author (PDF byline): Jessica K Lu. Year 2026. *PLOS Digital Health* 5(9):e0001691. DOI 10.1371/journal.pdig.0001691. PMCID PMC13614584.
- Device actually deployed (Accelerometer protocol). "The ActiGraph wGT3X-BT triaxial accelerometer (firmware Version 1.9.2) (Ametris, LLC, Florida, USA) was used to monitor physical activity (PA) and sedentary behaviour (SB) for seven consecutive days from each visit." Matches the queue.
- Verdict: INCLUDE. Operational feasibility is the stated aim. Device concerns per participant-visit, wear adherence per visit, dropout reasons and a wear-to-dropout model are all reported.
- Existing Module 3 profile covering this cohort: none.

Extraction
- Design. Single-site double-blind RCT of alpha-ketoglutarate, NCT05706389, NUS-IRB 2021-946. This paper is an exploratory operational analysis.
- Funding/COI. Lien Foundation gift. No competing interests. Compensation not reported.
- Population. Singapore, healthy adults aged 40 to 60 with older DNA methylation age.
- Funnel. 120 randomised. 119 with device data (one lost the device at baseline and withdrew). Device data n 119, 110, 102, 98 by visit. Dropout 20 of 120 (17%). Screening funnel only in Fig 1 image.
- Duration. 9 months, 7 days wear after each of 4 visits.
- Deployment. Provisioned, mail-back via SingPost on day 8, no reminders, WhatsApp and email support.
- Completeness. Valid day is at least 960 minutes. Adherent visit is at least 4 valid days. Adherent 81% to 97% by arm and visit (Table 3). Median wear across all participant-days 1400 min (IQR 1245 to 1440).
- Technical failure modes (Table 2). 6 participants, 8 of 432 participant-visits. 1 refusal for appearance (3 visits), 2 device losses, 1 skin irritation, 1 rash, 1 strap odour.
- Dropout reasons (Table 4). Work or schedule 10, lost to follow-up 4, product 2, non-compliance 2, burden 1, medical 1.
- Wear and dropout (Table 5). HR 0.998 per minute of daily wear, HR 0.208 for male.
- Not reported. Compensation, recruitment channels, hardware malfunction counts, whether losses occurred in the post.
