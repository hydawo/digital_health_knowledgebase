Wearable Data Atlas

What fifteen consumer, athletic and research-grade wearable platforms actually let a study collect, access and export: access model, sensors, raw-signal specifications, API mechanics, study operations, participant burden, cost, compliance posture and validation evidence.

The legacy Fitbit Web API is turned off on 30 October 2026 (support ended 30 September); the Google Health API is apparently open to new projects again (Corroborated; the 2026-10-10 notice was removed by 2026-10-19), with Google's restricted-scope review; Garmin has paused new developer-programme access requests since spring 2026 (Corroborated); and from 12 October 2026 participants with new Withings accounts need a paid Withings+ membership to share data with a free-plan app.

TABLE 10

Meaningful differentiators

What matters about each platform, and the limitation that most often rules it out.

Apple

No vendor in the data path, on-device history at enrolment (participants can cap it at 30 days from iOS 27 unless the app holds the Research Entitlement), and SensorKit PPG, ECG and accelerometer samples for approved studies. Limitation: iPhone only; no server API; little study tooling beyond the Study App Template.

Fitbit / Google

Deepest validation literature and a 59,000-participant open dataset. Limitation: legacy API off 30 Oct 2026; Google Health API apparently open to new projects again (C, 2026-10-21).

Garmin

Weeks of battery, no subscription required, and Enhanced BBI with per-beat confidence. Limitation: new direct access paused (C); raw accelerometry via the enterprise SDK only.

Oura

Nightly RMSSD and temperature from a ring people keep on overnight; best resting HR/HRV in Dial 2025. Limitation: membership required; API terms limit retention and bar AI training.

WHOOP

Charged on the wrist, so no charging gap, and no screen. Limitation: derived scores only through the API (2023 Unite raw route, status Unclear); terms bar permanent copies and AI training.

Samsung

Raw PPG (green, IR, red), raw ECG at 500 Hz, IBI and raw EDA at 1 Hz (Watch8) from a mainstream watch, with no Samsung server in the path for sensor data. Limitation: Galaxy only; Samsung may end the licence without cause on 30 business days' notice; Research Stack status Unclear, no code since Oct 2024.

Polar

Raw ECG from a $104.95 H10 and 22-bit PPG from Verity Sense via a free, source-available SDK, with no cloud, approval or fee. Limitation: session devices; Polar can end the SDK licence on 90 days' notice.

Withings

Cellular devices that remove the participant's phone from the data path, plus blood pressure and an under-mattress sleep mat. Limitation: raw data by contract; Withings+ from 12 Oct 2026.

Empatica

Raw wrist EDA with an FDA-cleared EDA parameter and a published-price academic plan with study management. Limitation: sensor mode fixed per academic account (PPG and EDA only in 2 of 5 modes); no streaming.

Ametris

The actigraphy reference standard, with regulated-trial operations in 70+ countries. Limitation: non-public pricing; corporate transition.

Axivity / GENEActiv

Open firmware, software and hardware (Axivity), no vendor in the data path (Axivity; GENEActiv by USB), AX3 to 3,200 Hz, and comparability with UK Biobank. Limitation: movement, light and temperature only, nothing known until return except GENEActiv 1.3 adherence; Activinsights' terms of sale (clause 24) bar publishing about the product without its consent.

Movesense

Custom sensor firmware with no licence cost, 512 Hz ECG, a high-rate IMU (maximum is a vendor conflict), and EU Class IIa on the MD with certified firmware (custom firmware and Class IIa are alternatives, not a combination). Limitation: proprietary evaluation licence; no FDA clearance; session or multi-day wear (Flash logs up to 5 days of ECG).

Ultrahuman

Documented raw ring PPG (typically 50 Hz), and ring plus CGM in one API (R). Limitation: raw PPG limited to the developer's own recordings; Ring AIR not sold in the US; API Agreement bars research against Ultrahuman; no validation located.

Biostrap / Sensor Bio

Raw PPG, IBI and accelerometer up to 100 Hz with per-organisation sampling (V2, vendor specification); the leg-worn Activity Pod is legacy. Limitation: prices not public; a 15,000 samples/s press claim conflicts with the 100 Hz specification; raw-export tier gating conflicts; validation counts vendor-compiled.

Verily Study Watch

ECG and EDA and PPG and inertial in one FDA-cleared research device, with up to a week of onboard raw storage (C). Limitation: device program wound down 2025; status conflict (R).

Labfront

Published pricing (as do Fitrockr, Terra and Thryve), a free 5-participant tier and EMA at every level. Limitation: no Fitbit.

Fitrockr

Published per-device pricing across many brands, with Garmin research grants. Limitation: vendor compliance claims only (HIPAA "certified" with no certifier; ISO 27001 is the hosting centre's); BAA newly offered 2026-09-19 (R); surveys and raw data are paid add-ons.
