# Module 4 — Methods and Reviews

**Status:** created 2026-09-03 as a filing home, not yet researched. Ten papers, all reviews or
position papers, moved here from the Module 2 catalogue.

## What this module is

Modules 1 and 2 describe technologies. Module 3 describes what happened when those technologies were
deployed. This module holds the papers that are about neither a technology nor a deployment: reviews,
scoping and systematic reviews, consensus statements, position pieces and methods papers about how
digital health research is done, regardless of platform or device.

## Scope

In: reviews and meta-analyses of digital phenotyping or wearable research, consensus and ethics
statements, perspective and framework papers, and methods papers whose subject is research practice
(missing-data handling, study design, validation frameworks) rather than one platform's own analytics.

Out: a platform's own analytics stack (Beiwe's step-count and GPS-imputation papers stay in Module 2
next to the platform), device-accuracy validation (Module 1), deployment reports (Module 3), and
statistics papers with no digital-health content.

## Why it exists

The Module 3 coverage pass on 2026-09-03 rejected twelve papers as "review" or "architecture" with
nowhere to file them, and the weekly literature scan will keep producing more. A named home stops them
being dropped or wrongly attributed to a platform.
