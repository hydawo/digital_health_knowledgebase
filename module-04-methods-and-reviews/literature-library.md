## Baseline search, 2026-09-03

Europe PMC REST (`/search`, `sort=P_PDATE_D desc`, `resultType=core`, page size 1000), every query restricted to `FIRST_PDATE:[2019-01-01 TO 2026-12-31]` and, for the ten topic queries, `OPEN_ACCESS:y`, each combined with a review filter (`PUB_TYPE:"Review"` or a title word from review, scoping, systematic, meta-analysis, consensus, framework, recommendations, guideline, perspective, roadmap, tutorial, best practices, checklist, statement). Hit counts: Q1 digital phenotyping in title or abstract, 127; Q2 wearable adherence, engagement or retention in title, 9; Q3 missing data, non-wear or data quality with a wearable or smartphone term in abstract, 78; Q4 ethics, consent or privacy in title with a sensing term in abstract, 55; Q5 validation, verification, fit-for-purpose or evaluation in title with a digital biomarker or wearable term in abstract, 62; Q6 reporting, checklist, guideline, CONSORT, SPIRIT, STROBE or TRIPOD in title with a sensing term in abstract, 109; Q7 equity, disparities, representativeness, digital divide, bias, inclusion or fairness in title, 16; Q8 EMA, experience sampling or ambulatory assessment in title with compliance, adherence, engagement, meta-analysis, review, recommendations or reporting in title, 113; Q9 passive sensing, mobile sensing, smartphone sensing or smartphone-based in title, 108; Q10 wearable, digital health or mHealth in title with privacy, security, data governance, data sharing or data protection in title, 29. Total 706 hits, 646 unique records after de-duplication. A supplementary pass without the OA restriction added a DOI lookup for the V3 paper (1 hit) and title-only queries for equity (225), missing data (53), wearable adherence reviews (19), digital phenotyping ethics (18) and digital biomarker validation (114), from which six further papers were taken. All abstracts were screened by hand; 38 open-access papers were kept and downloaded, 3 paywalled papers are recorded without a PDF. Exclusions followed the README scope: platform-specific analytics, device-accuracy validation, deployment reports, protocols, congress abstracts and general digital-health papers with no sensing content.
