# Cardiac seed, Lane A: consensus, guidance, standards and evaluation frameworks

Lane A covers consensus statements, position papers, clinical guidance, measurement standards and
validation or evaluation frameworks for measuring cardiac signals with wearables and phones: heart
rate, HRV, single- and multi-lead wearable ECG, PPG-based AF detection and notification, and cuffless
blood pressure. Lane B (metric definitions, device-agnostic algorithms, pooled accuracy reviews and
meta-analyses, physiological and signal limits) is seeded separately.
