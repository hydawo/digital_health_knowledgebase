# Physical activity seed, Lane A: consensus, guidance, standards, reporting and evaluation frameworks

Lane A covers consensus statements, position papers, guidance, measurement standards, reporting and
data-processing recommendations, and validation or evaluation frameworks for measuring physical
activity, steps, energy expenditure, sedentary behaviour, 24-hour movement behaviour and
cardiorespiratory fitness with wearables and phones. Lane B (metric definitions and cut-points,
device-agnostic algorithms and open methods, pooled accuracy meta-analyses, physiological and signal
limits) is seeded separately.

## 6. Gaps

- Cardiorespiratory fitness has almost no guidance of its own: the only wearable-specific
  recommendations sit inside the INTERLIVE VO2max meta-analysis (Molina-Garcia 2022,
  [10.1007/s40279-021-01639-y](https://doi.org/10.1007/s40279-021-01639-y), CC BY, not in the KB),
  left to Lane B because it is mainly a meta-analysis. If Lane B does not take it, it belongs here.
- No ISO or IEC standard for pedometers or activity monitors was found; national standards such as
  Japan's pedometer standard were not searched. CTA-2056-A and CTA-2074 are sold, so their test
  protocols and thresholds were not read.
- Most consensus on sedentary behaviour terminology (SBRN) and on step and MVPA cut-points is
  definitional and was left to Lane B; no ACSM position stand specific to wearables was found.
