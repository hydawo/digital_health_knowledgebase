# Module 5, Mental health: literature library

Measurement science for mental health with wearables and phones, across devices and platforms:
which constructs (mood, depression and anxiety severity, stress, psychosis relapse risk, social
behaviour) passive and active signals can measure, how features are defined, how well they track
validated clinical scales, and where they fail. Scope is in [`../README.md`](../README.md).

Boundary with Module 4: general research practice (EMA adherence, missing data, ethics) stays in
Module 4. This section holds papers about measuring a mental-health construct. Boundary with
Module 2: a platform's own feature pipeline stays with the platform.

PDFs are in [`literature/`](literature/). OA status markers follow the Sleep catalogue:
**Verified OA**, **OA but not obtained this pass**, **Paywalled**, **Free to read, not stored
(licence)**. Only openly licensed PDFs are stored, because this repo is public.

**Seeded 2026-10-23** from two search lanes (consensus, validation frameworks, EMA measurement and reporting; features, methods and datasets, pooled evidence and validity limits). Search logs are in [`_seed/`](_seed/). 53 papers: 15 PDFs on disk, 13 free to read but not stored for licence reasons, 21 free but blocked to scripts (listed in [`_hand-download.md`](_hand-download.md)), 4 paywalled. Ten strong reviews and methods papers already in Modules 2 and 4 were deliberately not re-catalogued; they are on the hub.

Papers on mental-health measurement that live in other modules are listed in [`hub.md`](hub.md).
