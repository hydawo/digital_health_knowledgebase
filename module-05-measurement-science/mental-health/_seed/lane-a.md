# Mental health seed, Lane A: consensus, guidance, frameworks and reporting

Lane A covers consensus statements, position papers, guidance, measurement and validation frameworks
and reporting recommendations about measuring mental-health constructs (mood, depression and anxiety
severity, stress, psychosis and relapse risk, suicidality, bipolar states, social behaviour) with
smartphone passive sensing, wearables and EMA. Lane B (feature definitions, device-agnostic methods,
pooled association and prediction meta-analyses, construct validity limits) is seeded separately.
General research practice (EMA adherence, missing data, ethics, generic V3, generic reporting) stays
in Module 4; a single platform's own methods stay in Module 2.
