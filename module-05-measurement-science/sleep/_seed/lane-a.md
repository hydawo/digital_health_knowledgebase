# Sleep seed, Lane A: guidance, consensus, standards and evaluation frameworks

Lane A covers consensus statements, position papers, guidelines, standards and evaluation frameworks
for measuring sleep with wearables, consumer sleep technology, actigraphy and phones. Lane B (metric
definitions, device-agnostic algorithms, pooled accuracy reviews, physiological limits) is seeded
separately.

## 5. Gaps noticed

- No phone-specific guidance on measuring sleep from smartphones turned up. The nearest papers
  are reviews (Aledavood 2019, already in Module 2), so phone sleep sensing currently has no
  guidance-level anchor in this module. No public IEEE standard for consumer sleep trackers was found
  either; the CTA/NSF 2052 series is the only formal standard.
- Left out as borderline, worth a second look: Carney 2012 consensus sleep diary (10.5665/sleep.1642,
  a self-report standard, not framed as the device reference), Walsh 2021 athlete sleep consensus
  (10.1136/bjsports-2020-102025), WFSBP insomnia biomarker consensus 2023 (10.1080/15622975.2023.2171479),
  Galland 2014 pediatric actigraphy challenges (10.3389/fpsyt.2014.00099), and de Zambotti 2019
  MSSE review (10.1249/mss.0000000000001947), which is more review than guidance.
- Rows S-A02 and S-A03 have no abstract and their full text was not read this pass, so their
  descriptions are thin and should be checked once the PDFs are in hand.
