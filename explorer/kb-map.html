Knowledge Base Map

A research library on digital health tools for studies. It answers one question: if a study team picks a given wearable or phone platform, what data can they actually get, and how have real studies gone with it? It has four modules, a weekly robot that finds new papers, a list of open questions, and two web pages you browse it through.

1 · The whole system

What has been built

Read left to right. Facts come in from the left, get filed into one of four modules, the loose ends get written down in the bookkeeping files, and you see the result through the pages on the right.

2 · The weekly robot

What the scan does, and what went wrong

Every Monday a cloud routine searches for papers published since its last run. It writes Module 1 and 2 papers straight into their libraries. For Module 3 it only adds candidates to a queue, because a study's numbers have to be checked in the full text. It then commits and is supposed to push to main.

Fixed today: all six runs are merged into main (commit cefb2f3). That brought in 15 Module 1 papers, 2 Module 2 papers and 16 Module 3 candidates, with no duplicates. Two papers one run queued and another rejected went into the queue with both verdicts on the row. Not fixed yet: the routine will keep using side branches until its settings change, and it still can't download PDFs.

3 · The open questions

What "Q120" and the rest mean

Whenever research hits something it can't settle (a price that isn't published, two papers that disagree, a paper behind a paywall, a call only you can make), it goes into shared/unresolved-questions.md with a number. Numbers run 1 to 125 in the order they came up. When I say "Q120", I mean row 120 of that file. The file prints the numbers without the Q, which is why my search missed them this morning.

On 9 October the file was reorganised by who can close each question, and all 34 questions a research session could answer were researched. 20 were closed outright. 83 remain open, and only 4 of those need you.

Open questions by who can close them. Bar length is to scale; longest is 55

The 55 vendor questions are not work to do. They are the list to send a vendor before a study signs with them: unpublished prices, whether they will sign a HIPAA agreement, API limits.
